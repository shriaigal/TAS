About {{ APP_NAME }}

The Traffic Analytics System (TAS) is an intelligent AI-powered platform developed to analyze traffic conditions, predict travel time, and optimize routing using Machine Learning, graph-based algorithms, and real-time analytics visualization.

🎯

Vision

To build an intelligent and scalable traffic analytics platform that improves travel planning through AI-driven route optimization and predictive traffic analysis.

🚀

Objective

  • ✅ Predict accurate Estimated Time of Arrival (ETA)
  • ✅ Analyze traffic conditions dynamically
  • ✅ Generate optimized and alternative routes
  • ✅ Improve routing efficiency using graph algorithms
  • ✅ Provide interactive traffic analytics visualization

How TAS Works

TAS combines Artificial Intelligence, route optimization algorithms, and analytics systems to generate intelligent traffic predictions and optimized routing recommendations.
